About me

How a lockdown hobby turned into a creative career path.

I'm Jibraj Karki, a graphic designer, content creator and video editor from Kathmandu, Nepal.

My YouTube journey began during the lockdown, right after I finished Class 10. I had very little equipment and almost no editing experience, but I enjoyed communicating and sharing useful information with people.

Over time I taught myself content creation, video editing and how to keep an audience engaged. That effort grew my channel, The Zivraj, to more than 55,000 subscribers. I'm still learning, still improving my creative skills, and still building my personal brand.
