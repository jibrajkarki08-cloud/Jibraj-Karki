I'm Jibraj Karki, a creative professional from Kathmandu, Nepal, working in graphic design, video editing and digital content creation.

I began my YouTube journey at a young age, starting with limited equipment and little editing experience. Step by step, I learned how to create videos, communicate ideas and connect with an audience.

Today, with a community of more than 55,000 subscribers, I continue to develop my creative skills and explore new opportunities. My goal is to create work that is useful, engaging and memorable.
